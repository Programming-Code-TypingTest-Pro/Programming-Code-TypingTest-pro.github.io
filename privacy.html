Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Programming Typing Speed Test, accessible from our main web application, user privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by our platform and how we utilize it.

🔒 Summary: We respect your privacy. Our core web tool requires zero account creation, no registration, and no collection of sensitive personal data to practice typing.

1. Information We Collect

Our website is designed as a free utility tool for developers and students. We do not require users to register or provide personal identification details to use our services.

  • Non-Personal Data: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, residential address, or phone number unless you voluntarily submit it via our Contact page.
  • Log Files: Like most standard web servers, our system follows standard procedures of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, and referring/exit pages. This information is not linked to any personally identifiable details.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Our website uses 'Cookies' to enhance user experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us remember user preferences (such as Dark Mode settings, Timer toggles, or Keyboard preferences) to optimize performance.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • Google AdSense uses advertising cookies to serve relevant ads based on prior visits.
  • Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.
  • We do not sell, trade, or rent any user information to third-party marketing companies.

4. Third-Party Links & External Web Services

Our website may contain links to external sites or developer resources. Please note that we are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party websites. We encourage users to read the privacy statements of any other site that collects personal information.

5. Data Security & Transparency

We take reasonable administrative and technical security measures to maintain data integrity and safeguard our platform. Our website operates strictly for general educational and practice purposes and does not knowingly collect data from children or vulnerable users.

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. We reserve the right to amend or update this document periodically. Any revisions will be reflected directly on this page with an updated effective date.
