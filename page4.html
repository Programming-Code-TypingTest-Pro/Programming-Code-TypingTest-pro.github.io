⚡ Pro Tips & Secret Tricks (Speed Boosters)

  • 💡 Read Ahead While Typing: Avoid memorizing syntax blindly. Always read 3 to 4 words ahead in your mind while typing the current line.
  • 💡 Accuracy Always Beats Speed: Pressing Backspace wastes the time it takes to type 2 to 3 words. Always target 98%+ accuracy.
  • 💡 Touch Typing Technique: Keep your fingers anchored on the home-row keys (ASDF JKL;) and maintain your eyes on the screen.
  • 💡 Master Special Characters: Programming relies heavily on symbols like ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) _ + { } | : " < > ?. Practice them specifically.
  • 💡 Learn IDE Shortcuts: In real-world software engineering, mastering multi-cursor selection, line duplication, and autocomplete shortcuts supercharges overall output.
